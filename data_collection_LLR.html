Data Collection of Vascular Procedures

Lower Limb Revascularisation (open surgical revascularisation and endovascular revascularisation)

Planned surgeries asscoiated with Lower Limb Revascularisation include -

  1. Angioplasty
  2. Bypass
  3. Embolectomy
  4. Endarterectomy

Angioplasty locations are Aorta, Right and Left leg. For each location vaues are stored on -

  • Repair - "Angioplasty";"Stent";"DCB";"DCS"
  • Lesion - "TASC A";"TASC B";"TASC C"
  • Outcome - "Success";"Residual Stenosis";"Failure"
  • Treatment after Failure - "Open Surgery";"Bypass";"Limb Loss"

Operation names - a selection of 1 or more procedures delivered for inclusion in the audit report. Angioplasty operation names may be edited. They currently include -
"Iliac angioplasty - no stent", "Iliac angioplasty - stent", "Popliteal angioplasty - no stent", "Popliteal angioplasty plus stent(s)", "Superficial femoral angioplasty - no stent", "Superficial femoral angioplasty plus stent(s)", "Tibial angioplasty - no stent", "Tibial angioplasty plus stent(s)"

Vascular access sites included are -

Inflow, Outflow - "Aorta", "Axillary artery", "Common Femoral artery", "Common Iliac", "External Iliac", "Popliteal – above knee", "Popliteal – below knee", "Profunda Femoral artery", "Superficial Femoral", "Superficial Femoral artery", "Tibial / Pedal"

Operation names - a selection of 1 or more procedures delivered for inclusion in the audit report. Bypass operation names may be edited. They currently include -
"Aorto - bifemoral bypass", "Axillo - femoral bypass", "Femoro - femoral bypass", "Femoro - pedal bypass", "Femoro - popliteal above knee bypass", "Femoro - popliteal below-knee bypass", "Femoro - tibial bypass", "Ileo - femoral bypass", "Popliteal - pedal bypass"

Operation names - a selection of 1 procedure delivered for inclusion in the audit report. Endarterectomy operation names may be edited. They currently include -
"Lower limb embolectomy any kind"

Operation names - a selection of 1 or more procedures delivered for inclusion in the audit report. Endarterectomy operation names may be edited. They currently include -
"Aortic Endarterectomy", "Femoral endarterectomy ( includes common femoral, superficial femoral, and profunda )", "Iliac endarterectomy", "Popliteal endarterectomy", "Tibial endarterectomy"
